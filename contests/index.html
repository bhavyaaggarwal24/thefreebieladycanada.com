How to get McDonald's Monopoly pieces without buying food

The contest has a free mail-in way to play (no purchase necessary). From the official game details:

  1. Put a self-addressed envelope with enough return postage inside a postage-paid outer envelope.
  2. Write your full return address on the outer envelope and mail it to the address in the official rules (mcdpromotion.ca/rules).
  3. Send each request in its own outer envelope. It must be postmarked by Nov 2, 2026 and received by Nov 9, 2026.
  4. Each valid request gets 2 game pieces (4 stamps), while supplies last.
  5. Enter the codes in the McD's app by Nov 23, 2026, 11:59 pm local time.

Read the official rules before mailing; they have the exact address and any limits per person or household.

Contest safety in Canada
  • Real Canadian contests often ask a skill-testing question before you can claim a prize. That's normal.
  • You never pay a fee to claim a prize. A message asking for money or card details is a scam.
  • Enter only on the official app or website named on each card.
